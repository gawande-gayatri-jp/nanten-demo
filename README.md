# Nanten: demo edition

Nanten is a modelling and decision platform for the Japanese power market: price, curtailment and
generation forecasts, PPA valuation and FIT-to-FIP conversion, storage, balancing and hedging, on one
engine.

This is the public demo edition of the prototype:

| Page | File | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | `index.html` | Demo edition of the main prototype |
| `/japan` | `japan.html` | Demo edition of the Japan version |

Open in the demo edition: every product's overview page, plus Market Analytics (Price explorer, Area
spreads), BRP (Balancing simulator), Fuel to Market (Fuel inputs, Merit order), Charge Desk (Portfolio
dashboard, Assets & curtailment, BESS optimizer) and the IPP Trading Suite (Hedge products, BESS
uplift). The Workspace and the other modules are shown blurred and locked; downloads and exports are
disabled. All figures are synthetic.
